+ New Orleans +

+ Leo Hererra: Spreading the gay Gospel down in New Orleans [Butt] "Southern Decadence is is a 50-year-old, five-day party in New Orleans, and the largest LGBTQ celebration in the American South. It’s like a darker, sexier sibling to Pride."

You talk about the relationship between cruising etiquette and southern hospitality. What’s the link?
"Southern hospitality isn’t just manners, it’s a survival tactic. Getting to know your neighbors can keep you mutually protected in places that experience so much climate disaster. The same goes for cruising. The reason most of us try to not trash the cruising spots at a park, or follow rules of consent in a darkroom, it’s to keep our spaces protected and our neighbors safe."
