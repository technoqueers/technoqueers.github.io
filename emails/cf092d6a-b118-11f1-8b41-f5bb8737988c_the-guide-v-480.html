+ Art +

+ This new Times Square arts exhibition explores the neighborhood’s seedy history of sex clubs and debauchery [TimeOut] "Live Sex Acts opens Friday, September 18, at Times Square Space, bringing together photographs and artworks by Vivienne Maricevic, Scott Ewalt and Alix Vernet. Despite its name, the show features no live performances. It’s an 18-and-over exhibition about the adult venues that flourished from the late 1960s through the mid-1980s—and what happened when they disappeared."

"The exhibition also asks what those spaces meant to the people who used them: its organizers describe clubs and darkened theaters as places where queer New Yorkers could gather beyond the scrutiny of mainstream culture and where sexuality, art and activism sometimes met. The venues sold sex, but they also held communities and creative work that couldn’t easily find room elsewhere."
