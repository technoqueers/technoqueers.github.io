+ F*ggots Are Women +

+ Sniffies Interviews Kay Gabriel [Sniffies Cruising Confessions]

Sniffies: You're known for championing the phrase "f*ggots are women": can you explain the theory behind the statement and what it means to you?

KG: The phrase originated as a mishearing. My collaborator and the other half of my brain [Patrick Dedauw] stuck his hands in the air and said "f*ggots are winning!" [because he got something that I wanted] and I was halfway out of the room and I misheard it, and I heard him say "f*ggots are women" and I said yes, f*ggots are women. And then it just kind of became something like a philosophy and a mantra. [...] It's not actually a theory of gender, it's a funny thing to say to disarm each other into being in relation. That is actually the point of saying "f*ggots are women".
